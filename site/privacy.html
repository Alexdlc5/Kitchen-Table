Privacy policy

Effective 20 October 2026. This policy covers the Kitchen Table app for iPhone and iPad and this website.

The short version: no ads, no tracking, no account. Your games and settings stay on your device. We only receive anonymous usage numbers and crash reports if you say yes, and you can change your mind at any time.

What stays on your device

Your games in progress, scores, statistics, the fair deal ledger, your settings and the caregiver PIN are saved on your iPhone or iPad only. We never see them. Deleting the app deletes them.

Anonymous usage numbers and crash reports (only if you say yes)

The first time you play, the app asks "Help make The Kitchen Table better?". Until you answer yes, nothing is sent. If you say yes, the app uses Google's Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to send us:

  • how the app is used, such as which game was started, which rules preset and computer level, and when a game ends;
  • crash reports and performance data: what the app was doing when it crashed, the device model, the iOS version and the app version;
  • a random identifier made for this install, so that one person's crashes count once. It is not your name, email, Apple Account or the device's advertising identifier, and we cannot tie it to you.

Your answer itself is recorded once, with nothing else attached. You can turn this off at any time in Options › Share anonymous usage numbers; from then on nothing more is sent. Google processes this data for us under its Firebase terms. We keep usage numbers for no longer than 14 months; crash reports are kept for 90 days. We use this data only to fix problems and improve the game, never for advertising, and we never sell it.

Purchases

Purchases go through Apple's App Store; we never see your name, payment card or Apple Account. To know which games and table themes you own on each of your devices, the app uses RevenueCat, a purchase service. RevenueCat receives the App Store's record of your purchases and a random identifier for the app, not your name or email. It uses them only to confirm and restore what you bought.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, the app sends your scores (for example, games won) to Apple's Game Center leaderboards. Apple handles Game Center under its own privacy policy; we see only what Game Center shows every player. Playing with family on two devices also goes through Game Center, and only to the people you invite.

No ads, no tracking

The app contains no advertising and no ad software. It does not track you across other companies' apps or websites, does not use the advertising identifier, and does not share data with data brokers.

This website

This site is hosted by GitHub Pages, which, like any web host, may keep its own server logs (such as IP addresses) under GitHub's privacy statement. The site sets no cookies and runs no analytics. The Check a deal page works entirely in your browser: the seed you type is never sent anywhere.

Children

The app is for everyone and does not ask anyone for personal information. Usage numbers are off until someone says yes, and a caregiver can lock settings and purchases behind a PIN.

Your choices and questions

You can turn usage numbers and crash reports off in Options at any time. Because we cannot tie that data to you, we cannot look it up by person, but if you have any question or request about your data, write to us on the help page and we will answer.

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its date. If a change means collecting anything new, the app will ask you first.
