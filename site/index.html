The Kitchen Table is the card games you grew up with, for iPhone and iPad: Hearts, Spades, Euchre, Oh Hell and Whist, with computer partners who play like real people and wait as long as you need.

  • Hearts is free, for good.

A fair deal you can check

Every hand is shuffled from a random seed and nothing else: not your score, your streak, or what you bought. Before the deal the app shows the seed's fingerprint, then you cut the deck. The score sheet prints the seed, and this site deals the same four hands from it, card for card, in your own browser.
