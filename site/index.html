Pull up a chair

The Kitchen Table is the card games you grew up with, for iPhone and iPad: Hearts, Spades, Euchre, Oh Hell and Classic Whist (partnership whist, no bidding), with computer partners who play like real people and wait as long as you need.

  • Hearts is free, for good.
  • One $6.99 unlock adds Spades, Euchre, Oh Hell, Classic Whist and every game we add later; Bid Whist is coming. You pay once and keep it, and Family Sharing covers your household.
  • No ads, ever. No subscription. No account to make.
  • Big cards, large text, a four-color deck and read-aloud, for anyone who wants them.

A fair deal you can check

Every hand is shuffled from a random seed and your cut. Your score, your streak and what you bought play no part. Before the deal the app shows the seed's fingerprint, then you cut the deck. The score sheet prints the seed and the cut, and this site re-deals any hand from its seed and cut, card for card, in your own browser.
