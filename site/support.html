Common questions

How do I unlock Spades, Euchre, Oh Hell and Classic Whist?

On the title screen, tap Games and rules and pick any locked game, or open Options › Unlock everything. It's one purchase, once, and it includes every game we add later.

I bought it, but on my new phone it's locked again

Tap Restore purchases on the title screen while signed in to the same Apple Account. Family members in your Family Sharing group can do the same.

Are there ads or a subscription?

No, and there never will be. Hearts is free; the rest is a one-time unlock; table themes are optional extras.

The cards are too small

Open Options and turn on Comfort mode for bigger cards and buttons and a slower table, or choose Large or Extra large text. A four-color deck makes diamonds blue and clubs green.

Can the game read the table to me?

Yes. It works with VoiceOver, and Options › Read the table aloud speaks every play with the phone's own voice and adds a Read button at the table.

How do I know the deal is fair?

Every score sheet shows the seed and the cut of that deal. Type them into Check a deal and you get the same four hands, card for card. The Fair deal page in the app also keeps a ledger of your hands next to what a fair deck gives on average.

I don't hear any sound

The game respects the ring/silent switch. To hear it with the switch on silent, turn on Options › Play sound when the phone is on silent.

Setting it up for someone else

The Caregiver page (link on the title screen) lets you set a PIN, lock settings and purchases, choose which games appear, and open straight into a game. If the PIN is forgotten, deleting and reinstalling the app clears it (with the saved scores); purchases come back with Restore purchases.

Do I need an account or the internet?

No account. The games play offline; you need a connection only to buy, restore, or use Game Center.
