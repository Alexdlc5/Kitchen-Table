Verify a deal

Every hand in Kitchen Table comes from a random 128-bit seed and your cut, and nothing else. Before the deal the app shows the seed's fingerprint; then you cut the deck. The score sheet prints all three. Type them here and this page deals the same four hands, using the same code as the app.

How the shuffle works

The seed's 128 bits seed a xoshiro128** generator (each 32-bit word mixed through SplitMix32 first). A Fisher-Yates shuffle then puts the cards in order, using rejection sampling so no card is favoured.

Your cut is mixed into the shuffle. With a cut, the cards are shuffled from the first 16 bytes of the SHA-256 of the seed's 16 bytes followed by one more byte, the cut, so each cut gives an unrelated deal. A cut of 0 (no cut) shuffles from the seed itself. The fingerprint is the first 16 characters of the SHA-256 of the seed's 16 bytes. The app shows it before you cut, so the seed was fixed before anyone knew your cut. Any SHA-256 tool gives the same answers; this page shows the bytes so you can check.

Cards are dealt one at a time, starting left of the dealer. In Hearts, these are the hands before passing. In Euchre, each player gets five and the last four are the kitty, its top card turned up. In Oh Hell, the next card after the hands is turned for trump. In Whist, the dealer's last card is trump.

The shuffle takes exactly two things: the seed and the number of cards. It can't see the score, your purchases, your winning streak or who is human. The full source is below and in the app's open test suite.

Can every order of the deck come up? No, and that's fine. A seed is one of 2128 (about 3.4×1038) numbers, and a deck can be ordered about 8×1067 ways, so most orders can never be dealt. Every seed is equally likely, and every card is equally likely to land in every seat.

Does this page prove the app ran this code? No page can. It proves that this seed and cut give these hands. That the app deals the same way is shown by its open source, which anyone can read, run and test. That is the real guarantee.
