Verify a deal

Every hand in Kitchen Table is shuffled from a random 128-bit seed and nothing else. The seed is printed on the score sheet after each hand. Paste it here and this page deals the same four hands, using the same code as the app.

How the shuffle works

The seed's 128 bits seed a xoshiro128** generator (each 32-bit word mixed through SplitMix32 first). A Fisher-Yates shuffle then puts the 52 cards in order, using rejection sampling so no card is favoured. Cards are dealt one at a time, starting left of the dealer. In Hearts, these are the hands before passing.

The deal function takes exactly two things: the seed and the number of cards. It can't see the score, your purchases, your winning streak or who is human. The full source is below and on the app's open test suite.
